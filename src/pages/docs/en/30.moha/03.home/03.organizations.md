# Organizations

An **organization** is the team space in Moha — think of it as a large shared office: everyone in the same organization puts their models and datasets here so the others can see them. The organization page is the resource overview of that office.

## How to get there

Click **Home** in the top navigation, then open the organization page in either of two ways:

1. Click the **Organization** button above the left menu.
2. Click an organization avatar in the **Joined Organization** section on the left.

![Organization page (System): the organization card and the Other organizations list on the left, the organization resources grouped into public / tenant-only / private in the middle](/assets/screenshots/moha/org-home-01.png)

Mind the **Created by me** switch above the list — turning it on leaves only what you created. System owns 4 public, 1 tenant-only and 1 private resource here, and every card is badged with its visibility.

## What the organization page looks like

| Area | What it shows you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization card on the left | The organization avatar, alias and description | Confirm which organization you are viewing |
| **Other Organizations** on the left | The other organizations you belong to | Click to switch to another organization |
| Type tabs at the top | Model / Dataset / Images / Space, each with a count | Click to switch the resource type |
| Resource area | All resources of the current type, split by visibility | Browse, search and open details |

## Browse the team's resources

1. Click the **Model**, **Dataset**, **Images** or **Space** tab at the top.
2. The resources below are split into three sections, each with a count after its title:
   - **Public resources**: visible to everyone.
   - **Internal**: visible only to members of this organization.
   - **Private**: visible only to the creator.
3. When a section has more resources, a **View all …** button appears under its title; click to expand and it changes to **Collapse …**.
4. To locate something quickly, type a name or keyword in the search box; click the refresh icon at the top right to fetch the latest list.

## See only what you created

There is a **Created by Me** switch above the resource area. Turn it on and all three sections show only the resources you created yourself, so you can confirm what you uploaded to the team.

## Switch organizations

In the **Other Organizations** section on the left, click the name of the target organization to switch to its page. When you belong to many organizations the list scrolls.

:::tip What organization and visibility really mean

- **Organization** is a team space whose members share repositories.
- **Visibility** is who can see a repository, in three levels: public, internal and private.

:::

## Confirm it worked

- After switching tabs the resource area changes accordingly, and the counts on the tabs match the real numbers.
- After turning on the **Created by Me** switch, the list contains only resources you created.
- Clicking any resource card opens its details page.

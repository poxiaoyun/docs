# Organizations

An **organization** is the team space in Moha — think of it as a large shared office: everyone in the same organization puts their models and datasets here so the others can see them. The organization page is the resource overview of that office.

## How to get there

Click **Home** in the top navigation, then open the organization page in either of two ways:

1. Click the **Organization** button above the left menu.
2. Click an organization avatar in the **Other Organizations** section on the left.

![Organization page: organization card and Other Organizations list on the left; type tabs, Visibility radios (Public/Internal/Private), Created by Me switch and 24 resource cards on the right](/assets/screenshots/moha/org-home-01.png)

Note the **Visibility** radio options and the **Created by Me** switch above the resource area — the first filters by visibility, the second leaves only what you created. Every card also carries its own visibility badge.

## What the organization page looks like

| Area | What it shows you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organization card on the left | The organization name, alias and description | Confirm which organization you are viewing |
| **Other Organizations** on the left | The other organizations you belong to, with a count in the title | Click to switch to another organization |
| Type tabs at the top | Model / Dataset / Images / Space, each with a count | Click to switch the resource type |
| Visibility filter | The **Public** / **Internal** / **Private** radio options | Keep only resources of one visibility level |
| **Created by Me** switch | Turning it on shows only resources you created | Quickly check what you uploaded to the team |
| Resource cards | The resources matching the current filters, 24 per screen | Search, open details, refresh |

When the organization has no resource of the selected type, the card area shows an empty state.

## Filtering by type and visibility

1. Click the **Model**, **Dataset**, **Images** or **Space** tab at the top.
2. Click one of the visibility options above the resource area:

| Option | What it filters to |
| --- | --- |
| **Public** | Resources that everyone can see |
| **Internal** | Resources only members of this organization can see |
| **Private** | Resources only the creator (and authorized members) can see |

3. To narrow further, type a name or keyword in the search box, or click **Advanced** in the top right, tick conditions such as classification and framework, then click **Apply filters**.
4. Click the refresh icon in the top right to fetch the list again.

Resources load one screenful at a time (24 cards); scroll to the bottom and the next batch loads automatically — there are no page numbers.

## See only what you created

There is a **Created by Me** switch above the resource area. Turn it on and the current type shows only the resources you created yourself; turn it off to see everything in the organization again.

The switch stacks with the visibility filter: choose **Internal**, then turn on **Created by Me**, and you see only the internal resources you created inside the organization.

## Switch organizations

In the **Other Organizations** section on the left, click the name of the target organization to switch to its page. When you belong to many organizations the list scrolls; if you belong to only one, the section shows **No Organizations**.

:::tip What organization and visibility really mean

- **Organization** is a team space whose members share repositories.
- **Visibility** is who can see a repository, in three levels: public, internal and private.

:::

## Confirm it worked

- After switching tabs the resource area changes accordingly, and the counts on the tabs match the real numbers.
- After picking a visibility option, every visibility badge on the cards shows the level you selected.
- After turning on the **Created by Me** switch, the list contains only resources you created.

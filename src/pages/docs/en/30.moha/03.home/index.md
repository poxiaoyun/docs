# Home page and personal workbench

The Moha home page is not a welcome poster — it is your **personal workbench**. The left column always holds your own things (Created by Me, My Favorites, Access Tokens, Announcements) plus the organizations you belong to, while the right side changes with what you click.

## How to get there

Click **Home** in the top navigation of Moha.

![The left column of the home page: the avatar and organization-home entry, the five items Overview / Created by me / Liked by me / Access token / Announcements, and the Joined organizations list below](/assets/screenshots/moha/home-overview-01.png)

This column only exists under the **Home** group; it collapses once you go to Models, Datasets or any other page. The number in brackets after **Joined organizations** is how many organizations the account belongs to, and clicking a tile jumps straight to that organization page.

## What the left column contains

| Area | What it shows you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name card at the top | Your name, email and avatar | Confirm which account is signed in |
| **Organization** button | Your current organization | Click to open that organization's resource page |
| Menu | Overview, Created by Me, My Favorites, Access Tokens, Announcements | Click any item to switch the right side to that page |
| Joined Organization | Every organization you belong to (shown as avatars) | Hover to see the name, click to open that organization's page |

If **Joined Organization** is empty it shows **No Organizations**, which means your account has not joined any organization yet. You can create one in [Account settings](/moha/quickstart/account).

## What the five pages show

| Menu | What this page shows you | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overview | Recommended resources, quick creation entries, platform announcements | [Home page overview](/moha/home/overview) |
| Created by Me | The models, datasets, images and Spaces you uploaded | [Resources I created](/moha/home/created-by-me) |
| My Favorites | The resources you have starred | [Liked by Me](/moha/home/liked-by-me) |
| Access Tokens | The token for the current session and its copy button | [Access tokens and announcements](/moha/home/token-announcements) |
| Announcements | All announcements published by the platform | [Access tokens and announcements](/moha/home/token-announcements) |

![Top-right avatar menu: avatar and account name, Home, Personal settings, the tenant (Demo project), plus Switch tenant and Sign out at the bottom](/assets/screenshots/moha/avatar-menu-01.png)

The menu has two layers: **Home / Personal settings** are personal, while the **tenant** row is the team. **Personal settings** opens the user centre, clicking the tenant name switches to that tenant's view, and **Switch tenant** at the bottom re-selects one when there is no default.

## Personal view and team view

- To see only **your own** resources, use **Created by Me** and **My Favorites**.
- To see everything owned by **the whole team**, click **Organization** to open the [organization page](/moha/home/organizations).

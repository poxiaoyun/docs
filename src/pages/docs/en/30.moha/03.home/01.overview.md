# Home page overview

This is the page you land on by default after opening Moha. It compresses "find a resource" and "create a resource" into one screen: the platform banner on top, four creation entries in the middle, and recommended models plus announcements below.

## How to get there

Click **Home** in the top navigation, then make sure the left menu is on **Overview**.

## The four blocks on this page

| Block | What it shows you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banner | Banner images for platform events or campaigns | A banner with a link opens that page when clicked; multiple banners rotate automatically |
| Quick creation | Four entries: Create Model, Create Dataset, Create Image, Create Space | Click one to go straight to the matching creation form |
| Recommended Model | A list of models the platform recommends | Browse the cards, click one to open its details |
| Announcements | Announcement titles and effective times | Click a title to read the full text, click **View All** for the archive |

## Create something quickly

1. In the four entries in the middle of the page, click the type you want: **Create Model**, **Create Dataset**, **Create Image** or **Create Space**.
2. The page jumps to the matching creation form. Choose an organization, fill in a name and submit.
3. After creation the resource appears in [Resources I created](/moha/home/created-by-me).

## Browse recommended models

1. In the **Recommended Model** block, click a card you are interested in to open the repository details.
2. A search box above the list lets you type a name or keyword to filter quickly.
3. The pager at the bottom of the list lets you move through more results.

To browse the full model library, use **Models** in the top navigation.

## Read announcements

1. In the **Announcements** block on the right, click any title to open a dialog with the full text.
2. Click **Close** in the dialog to go back.
3. To see every announcement, click **View All** at the bottom of the block.

## Confirm it worked

- After clicking a quick creation card, the address bar and the form title change to the matching creation page.
- After clicking an announcement title, a detail dialog opens showing the full content.
- After clicking a recommended model card, you enter that model's details page.

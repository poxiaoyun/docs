# My Favorites

This page gathers the resources you **clicked the heart icon on**, split into four types: models, datasets, images and Spaces. In the left menu it is called **My Favorites**, and this is the list it opens.

## How to get there

Click **Home** in the top navigation, then click **My Favorites** in the left menu.

## How to star a resource

1. Open the details page of any repository (a model, dataset, image or Space all work).
2. Find the **heart icon** button to the right of the title and click it.
3. The heart turns red, which means it is starred; click it again to remove the star.

![Liked by me: four tabs with the counts (Models 10 / Datasets 4 / Images 2 / Spaces 3) above the cards you liked](/assets/screenshots/moha/home-liked-01.png)

A liked card carries a solid red heart; click it again to unlike. Favourites are personal and never change a resource's visibility — nobody else can see what you liked.

## The blocks on this page

| Area | What it shows you | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Type tabs | Model / Dataset / Images / Space, each with the number you starred | Click to switch the type |
| Search box | Search your favorites by name or alias | Type a keyword and press Enter |
| Advanced search | Filters such as category and organization | Expand it and tick conditions |
| Refresh icon | A refresh button | Fetch the latest favorites again |
| Resource list | Cards for the repositories you starred in the current type | Click a card to open its details |

## Browse your favorites

1. Click the **Model**, **Dataset**, **Images** or **Space** tab at the top of the page.
2. The number next to each tab is how many you have starred in that type.
3. To find a specific favorite, type its name in the search box; use advanced search when you have many conditions.
4. When you find it, click the card to go straight back to that repository's details page.

## Confirm it worked

- The counts on the tabs match how many you actually starred.
- Under the title of every card in the list, the heart icon is solid red.
- If the list shows **No data**, you have not starred anything of that type yet.

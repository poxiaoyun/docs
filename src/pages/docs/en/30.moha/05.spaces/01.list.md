# Space List & Filters

The Space list is where you discover and pick Spaces. This page helps you search by name, narrow the range by domain and scene, sort by popularity, and finally open a Space to try it or read its code.

## Before you start

A signed-in Moha account is enough; browsing public Spaces needs no extra permission.

## Open the Space list

Click **Spaces** in the Moha top navigation.

![Space list layout: the four-step strip and create button, domain / scenario filters, search and sort, the Featured area, then the full list](/assets/screenshots/moha/spaces-list-01.png)

**Featured** is curated by the platform and overlaps the "All" list below — the same card can appear in both.

## Page structure

| Block | What you can do here |
| --- | --- |
| Filter area | Narrow the range by **Domain** and **Scene** |
| Toolbar | Search, see the total number of results, refresh, sort |
| **Featured** | Spaces the platform recommends |
| **All** | The card list of Spaces matching the current filters |

## Search

1. Type a Space name in the search box on the toolbar.
2. Press Enter to search.

The right side of the search box shows the total number of results for the current filters (for example "12 results"). Click the refresh button to reload.

## Sorting

Click the sort dropdown to switch between these three:

| Option | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Comprehensive | The default, ordered by the platform's overall weighting |
| Download Count | Most downloaded/used first |
| Like Count | Most liked first |

## Filter by domain and scene

1. In the filter area, find **Domain** or **Scene**.
2. Click an option and the list refreshes for that option immediately, returning to page 1.
3. Click the same option again to clear that filter.

**Scene** shows only its first few options by default; click **more** to expand the full set. The available domain and scene options are delivered by the platform — for example "Multimodal", "Computer Vision" and "Natural Language Processing" among the domains. A group is only shown when it has options.

## Featured and the All list

- The **Featured** section is independent of the **All** list and only shows Spaces the platform has marked as recommended, at most 8.
- Your search and filter conditions apply to the **Featured** section and the **All** list at the same time.

## Paging

The **All** list shows 12 Spaces per page; move to the next page to keep browsing.

![A Space repository's Overview tab: name, status, update time and three counters, then the git clone, sample app.py and commit steps under "Getting started"](/assets/screenshots/moha/repo-space-content-01.png)

A Space's **Overview** doubles as a quickstart: `git lfs install` → `git clone` → write `app.py` → `git push`. Ticking **Use my token** swaps the clone URL for a token-bearing one so private Spaces clone as well.

## Open a Space

Click any Space card to open its details page; the **Overview**, **File**, **Snapshot**, **Discussion & Feedback** and **Settings** tabs inside are described in [Repository details page](/moha/repository/detail).

## Confirm it worked

When Space cards appear in the list and the total number of results changes with your input, your search or filter has taken effect.

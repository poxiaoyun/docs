# Personal settings

Personal settings is where you manage "yourself": avatar and nickname, password, second factor, access keys,
and the look of the interface. It differs from tenant management — every item here affects only you and never touches your colleagues.

:::tip A comparison
Tenant management is like the company's administration desk, changing things for the whole company; personal settings are your badge and your desk, adjusting only your own things.
:::

## Before you start

- You can open it as soon as you are signed in; no tenant role is needed.
- It opens as a full page in the account application, and the back button at the top-left returns you to the page you came from.

## How to open it

1. Click your avatar in the top-right corner to slide out the drawer.
2. Click **Personal settings** in the drawer.
3. The page is titled **Personal settings**, with a row of four tabs underneath, starting on **General**.

![Top of personal settings: title, back button and four horizontal tabs (General / Security / Access keys / Preferences)](/assets/screenshots/account/profile-01.png)

## The four tabs

| Tab | What it holds | Detailed steps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **General** | Avatar, nickname, email, mobile number; the username is read-only | [General](/account/iam/profile) |
| **Security** | Change or set the password, multi-factor authentication, linking and unlinking third-party accounts | [Security](/account/iam/security) · [Third-party sign-in](/account/iam/linked-accounts) |
| **Access keys** | API keys for programs and SSH public keys for repositories | [API Key](/account/iam/api-key) · [SSH Key](/account/iam/ssh-key) |
| **Preferences** | Appearance mode (light/dark/system), color, language preference | [Preferences](/account/iam/preferences) |

:::info Email and mobile number live under General
They are not separate tabs. Open **General**, click **Change** on the matching row (or **Add** when nothing is bound yet),
and use the dialog to enter the new address and its verification code.
:::

## Two things that are not in personal settings

| What you want to do | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| Change company space | **Switch Tenant** at the bottom of the avatar drawer; see [Select / Register Tenant](/account/auth/select-tenant) |
| View tenant details or manage members | The **Tenant {tenant name}** row in the avatar drawer; see [Tenant Management](/account/iam/tenant) |

# Authentication

This group of pages does one thing: **confirm who you are and take you into the right company space**. Whether you are registering for the first time or signing in every day, this is where you sign in, recover a password, bind a second factor, and choose a tenant.

:::tip Signing in is really two small steps
First you prove who you are on the sign-in page, then you choose a company on the tenant selection page. If your account belongs to only one tenant, the second step happens automatically, so it can look like a single step.
:::

## Which pages you will use

| Page | When you use it |
| --- | --- |
| [Login](/account/auth/login) | Getting into the platform every day |
| [Registration](/account/auth/register) | Your first time, when you have no account yet |
| [Reset Password](/account/auth/reset-password) | You forgot your password |
| [Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)](/account/auth/mfa) | You want to add another layer of protection |
| [Roles and Permissions](/account/auth/roles) | You want to know what you can do, or check someone else's role |
| [Select / Register Tenant](/account/auth/select-tenant) | Choosing a company space after sign-in, or creating a new one |

## A few rules worth knowing

- After a successful sign-in you always pass through the tenant selection page first; if you belong to only one tenant, it enters that tenant automatically.
- The sign-in page has no "Remember Me"; you type your account and password every time.
- You must tick "I have read and agree to the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy." first, or the **Login** button will not respond.
- The sign-in page has two kinds of verification code (graphic and slider). Which one appears is decided by the platform.
- The reset password page cannot change your password by itself yet; ask an administrator to do it (see [Reset Password](/account/auth/reset-password)).
